Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Purpose

Personal Rclone Sync is a private OAuth client used only by its owner to connect rclone to the owner's Google Drive.

Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive files only when instructed by its owner for mounting, reading, writing, backup, and synchronization.

Storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. The application does not operate an external server or database that collects Google user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Credentials remain on the owner's device until the local rclone configuration is deleted or access is revoked through the owner's Google Account.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
